## What was searched

Source: `github.com/qiskit-community/qcode-discovery @ d6abdbd`,
`paper/2610.06623/weight5_paper.tex`. A dedicated weight-5-targeted
LLM-guided evolutionary search (OpenEvolve, "PBB-large" campaign,
`ell,m` swept across six lattices) over 4-tuples `(A, B, C, D)`, screened by
`k`, isotropy, a BP-OSD distance estimate, and a combined non-CSS gate
(`evaluation/noncss_gate.py`: `verify_not_lc_css` then `is_equivalently_css`

LC-equivalent to CSS under every tested Clifford class survive at all).
Standout candidates were then deep-MILP-verified per logical
(`evaluation/distance_milp.py`), and BLISS-deduped into equivalence classes.

The underlying code was first found as the connected half of a disconnected
parent presentation at `(ell=18, m=12)`, `n=432, k=8` (two isomorphic
components, `evaluation/connectivity.py:stabilizer_components` +
`components_isomorphic`); the parent's exact MILP distance (all 16 logicals

`results/weight5_pbb_large_v1_deep_milp_verify.jsonl`) transfers to each

(`results/weight5_pbb_component_certifications.jsonl`, match

both the uniform 36-assignment brute force and the non-uniform `{I,S}`/
`{H,HS}` affine-system check) and confirmed connected.

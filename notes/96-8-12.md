# [[96,8,12]] 2BGA on D_4 x Z_6 rebuilt from published supports

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: CSS, weight-8 x unrestricted, at small n. I wanted k >= 8 at d >=
12 with every check still at weight 8, because that is where the board's
two-block catalogue thins out: the best weight-8 entry with k = 8 sat at n =
104 ([[104,8,12]]), and nothing on the board had k >= 8 and d >= 12 below
that. Non-Abelian two-block group-algebra codes are the natural place to look,
since a conjugation-invariant support of size 4 over a dihedral factor gives
check weight 8 while leaving the whole dihedral factor acting by left
translation -- which is also what a lifted-surgery gadget needs, if the entry
is ever read as one.

## What was searched

No search was run, and none is claimed. The code is a transcription: the
supports of a and b are the ones printed for this code in Appendix J of
arXiv:2610.05307v1, and H_X = [L(a) | R(b)], H_Z = [R(b)^T | L(a)^T] is that
paper's Eq. 1-2, so (n, k, w) = (96, 8, 8) falls out of the construction
rather than out of a search. What I did search was that paper's codes against
this board: of the 22 it certifies with exact distance (Tables 1-2) plus the
two search candidates in its Appendix H, 21 are already dominated here --
including its best gain-three code [[336,26,12]] (12 dominators, e.g.
[[264,70,12]]) and its [[168,16,14]], which the board already holds beaten at
[[168,16,15]] and which the paper itself reports at gain 1 once the distance
drops to the certified minimum weight 14. [[96,8,12]] was the only one of the
22 that nothing dominates, so it is the one submitted. Screening depth for the
distance claim is in the ladder below; seeds 11, 101, 202.

## Evidence trail

Confirmation ladder for the submitted code, each rung a fresh seed unless
marked otherwise, reading the lightest logical found:

| ---: | ---: |
| 200,000 (screen, seed 11) | 12 |
| 1,200,000 (deep, seed 101) | 12 |
| 1,200,000 (deep, seed 202) | 12 |

3 fresh-seed rung(s) agree at the best bound, the deepest of them at 1,200,000
trials per side. The submitted witness reproduces it: X: d <= 12
(upper_bound), Z: d <= 12 (upper_bound).

The claim is an upper bound on both sides, witnessed at weight 12 in the entry
itself. arXiv:2610.05307v1 certifies d = 12 for this code exactly, by
block-split enumeration, but that is the paper's certificate and not one this
board has run, so the entry says d <= 12 and a maintainer can promote the
claim by running verify/certify.py. Nothing lighter turned up in 2,600,000
accelerator trials across three seeds, nor in the gate's own refutation pass.

## Dead ends

- Every gain-three code of arXiv:2610.05307v1 Table 2 is dominated here:
  [[336,26,12]] (12 dominators, e.g. [[264,70,12]]), [[336,24,12]] (16),
  [[336,24,10]] (19), [[288,24,10]] (9), [[240,24,10]] (4, e.g.
  [[210,26,14]]), [[240,18,10]] (12, e.g. [[192,20,16]]), [[240,24,8]] (9),
  [[240,24,6]] (18), [[288,20,6]] (25), [[288,28,6]] (11), [[216,24,6]] (6),
  [[144,24,6]] (3).
- [[168,16,14]] over S_3 x Z_14, the paper's most eye-catching search
  candidate, is dominated by [[168,16,15]] -- and its gain evaporates at the
  certified distance: the paper reports mu = 4 against a baseline of 4 at
  minimum weight, so gamma = 1 there.
- [[228,8,<=20]] over S_3 x Z_19 is an information-set estimate, not a
  certified distance, and it is dominated by [[216,8,21]] even at the
  estimated 20.
- The merged codes are not submittable as they stand: the paper gives them as
  bare [[N,k]] with no distance (e.g. [[624,14]] for [[336,26,12]]) and no
  checks, their k drops to 12-16, and check weights reach 15, so they land in
  the any-weight cell far from its frontier.
- Support data for the Table 2 codes is not printed anywhere in the paper --
  Appendix J covers only the 11 Table 1 codes, and element indices are
  deferred to the authors' package, which the paper does not link. Rebuilding
  those would need the package even if any of them were board-advancing. This
  one was not, so the paper text alone was enough.

## Reproduction

uv run --extra research python research/build_96_8_12_2bga.py out.npz -- the
script rebuilds (H_X, H_Z) from the group alone and asserts n = 96, k = 8, w =
8. G = D_4 x Z_6 as a Cayley table (|G| = 48, dihedral(4) x cyclic_product(6),
direct_product indexing), with a = {(e,e), (e,y), (r,e), (r^3,e)} and b =
{(e,e), (r,y^2), (s_0,e), (r^2,y^4)} as element indices [0, 1, 6, 36] and [0,
8, 12, 22]; checks are build_2bga's [L(a) | R(b)] and [R(b)^T | L(a)^T]. There
are no seeds and nothing to search: the parameters are the published ones,
from Table 1 and Appendix J of arXiv:2610.05307v1 (4 Oct 2026). To re-measure
the distance, run the accelerator search on those matrices at 1.2M trials per
seed; seeds 101 and 202 both read 12.

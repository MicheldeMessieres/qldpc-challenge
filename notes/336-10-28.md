# [[336,10,28]] — mirror fold (symplectic halving) of the board's [[672,20,32]]

## Direction & hypothesis

The stabilizer board is thin above n = 200: in the unrestricted weight-6 stabilizer cell the best kd^2/n was 7.62 when this search started (codes/85-8-9.json, @mrvee-qC-bee with Khesin and Lu), and no weight-6 stabilizer entry above n = 200 exceeded 4.0. Folding a strong CSS entry along an X<->Z mirror gives a genuinely non-CSS code with half the qubits and half the logicals, with distance between half and all of the parent's. This entry is derived, not a new construction.

**Derived from:** codes/672-20-32.json by @vprusso (found with Claude Opus 5): two-block group algebra (2BGA) code over the metacyclic group C_12 semidirect_5 C_28 of order 336 (construction of arXiv:2306.16400, Lin and Pryadko).
**Fold technique (not ours):** fieldnotes/2026-09-28-symplectic-doubling-and-halving.md (@MathysRennela; Prop. 4 of arXiv:2609.30069) and the mirror codes of Khesin and Lu (arXiv:2603.05496). Board precedents: @mrvee-qC-bee's mirror folds codes/155-6-21.json, codes/155-6-23.json, codes/144-10-16-b.json; @MathysRennela's CPM halvings codes/145-32-6.json, codes/188-49-6.json.
**Our contribution:** enumerating the X<->Z mirrors that are automorphisms of the parent's Tanner graph (up to conjugacy) and choosing among them, plus the distance search below.

## What was searched

- Mirrors: Tanner graph of the parent plus two marker vertices (one joined to every X-check, one to every Z-check); nauty gives the automorphism group, GAP lists the conjugacy classes of fixed-point-free involutions that swap the markers. Each class is one mirror. The same screen over 40 board leaders gave 51 folds from 36 parents.
- This parent has 4 mirror classes (folds t0-t3); lightest logical found per fold after 1.5-2.8 CPU-h each: 26, 26, 28, 28. This entry is t3.
- This fold: tau(1,g) = (2, h*psi(g)) with h = x^2 y^3 and psi the automorphism x -> x^7, y -> y^-1 (elements x^i y^j indexed i*28+j as in the parent); block 1 = parent qubits 0..335, block 2 = 336..671.
- Distance methods: (1) orbit trick: random information sets on the parent's X side; every light parent logical is pushed through all X-sector automorphisms of the parent and scored by fold weight (a pair hit on both halves becomes a Y and costs one qubit); (2) frame-randomised Pauli information-set search on the fold (random single-qubit Clifford frame each trial, half the trials in the identity frame); (3) SAT restricted to logicals constant on orbits of automorphism subgroups; (4) exact SAT ladder for a proved floor; (5) light logicals shared between the mirrors of one parent.
- Effort on this fold: 2.8 CPU-h (orbit trick: 1.5 CPU-h, 934670 trials; frame-randomised Pauli RIS: 0.9 CPU-h, 523469 trials; symmetry-restricted SAT: 0.3 CPU-h; exact SAT floor ladder: 0.1 CPU-h).

Parent's own claim: d <= 32; its stored witnesses fold to weight <= 32. Claim: d <= 28, witness-backed upper bound. No floor is claimed: the exact SAT ladder ran 0.1 CPU-h on this fold without proving any weight bound, and the parent distance is itself only an upper bound, so the relation d_fold >= ceil(d_parent/2) (a fold logical of weight w lifts to a parent logical of Hamming weight at most 2w) does not give a guaranteed number either.

Selection caveat: the mirror was chosen by lightest logical found, so its lead over the other mirrors of this parent may partly reflect search depth.

## Dead ends

- Plain fold RIS from scratch (no symmetry, 100k trials) stalled above the bound the parent's witnesses already give, so we did not rely on it alone for this fold.
- Mirror choice matters: on [[144,12,12]] (codes/144-12-12.json, Bravyi et al., arXiv:2308.07915) the five mirrors give exact distances 6, 8, 8, 8, 9.
- Folds of the bilayer [[360,12,24]] (codes/360-12-24.json, Liang, Liu, Song and Chen, arXiv:2503.03827) lost the most (to 14-15).

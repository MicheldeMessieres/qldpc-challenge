## Evidence trail

`B = 1 + x^3y^5 + x^{28}y^4`, perturbation `C = x^3y^3 + x^{25}y^3`,
`D = x^3y^5 + x^{25}y^5` (0-indexed exponent pairs: `A=[[3,2],[25,3],[25,4]]`,
`B=[[0,0],[3,5],[28,4]]`, `C=[[3,3],[25,3]]`, `D=[[3,5],[25,5]]`).
`n = 2·30·6 = 360`, `k = 4`, max check weight 8.

Same evidentiary structure as `[[360,12,20]]`: a deep per-logical MILP pass
(36,001.8s wall time) found a feasible incumbent of weight 24 for **every
one of the 8 logicals** (`logicals_incumbent: 8/8`), tightening the claim

Independent re-verification in *this* repository, using a third, unrelated

accelerator at 1,000,000 trials). **Independently found d<=24**, matching
the deep-MILP incumbent exactly and nowhere near the BP-OSD figure of 36.

Final claim here, honestly: `upper_bound`, `d<=24`.

# [[674,128,81]] cyclic generalized-bicycle code over Z_337 with k designed from a degree-64 divisor of x^337 - 1

## Direction & hypothesis

Cell: weight-9plus x unrestricted. The board's most efficient entries are
cyclic generalized-bicycle (GB) codes H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)] over Z_m with
m near 340 and check weight 24 to 32, each built on a designed divisor ideal
of x^m - 1 so that k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) is set before the distance is
measured. The designed-divisor sampler of PR #2790 reached that family from
Z_305 on its first six draws ([[610,122,73]], PR #2810); this campaign ran it
at scale over odd m from 281 to 345 to find Pareto-advancing codes in the
cell, whose headline is [[682,172,78]] at kd^2/n = 1534. This entry is the
best survivor: at n = 674 the board's [[674,128,80]] (a designed-divisor GB
at the same lift on a different degree-64 divisor, check weight 30, refuted
from 87 to 80 at 1,000,000,000 trials per side) is the only entry with
k >= 128 and d >= 80, and this entry reads 81 at 300,000,000 trials per side,
kd^2/n = 1246. The gain over it is one in distance at check weight 32
against 30, so it does not dominate that entry and both stand.

## What was searched

- Sampler: for each odd m, divisors g of x^m - 1 with 2 deg g in 100 to 200
  (about six per m from the irreducible-factor products), and for each g,
  sparse multiples a and b of g at weight 12 to 16 by a Prange search in the
  kernel of the circulant of the reciprocal check polynomial, plus Frobenius
  images of found words; pairs whose gcd exceeds g discarded. 4,815 draws in
  all: m = 305 (1,459), 331 (1,321), 337 (1,256), 329 (610), 287 (135),
  281 (133), 327 (105), 325 (53), 345 (43), 323 (30), 333 (12).
- Ladder (fast RIS, pair depth 32): 2,000 trials on every draw, keeping only
  draws whose reading would be Pareto-advancing at their (n, k) (4,179 of
  4,815 at that depth, which says how little a 2,000-trial reading means
  here); 20,000 trials on those (298 survivors); 200,000 trials (44
  survivors over m = 305, 323, 325, 327, 329, 337, 345); then 10,000,000
  trials per side on the six best by kd^2/n, run on a Perlmutter CPU node,
  seed 777. The three Z_327 members of that six collapsed from 82 to 28, 32
  and 40; the three Z_337 members held at 84 to 86.
- This code: g = 0x1b2e20f5df4de415d (degree 64), a with 16 terms
  (exponents 39, 69, 99, 146, 157, 173, 189, 216, 238, 251, 260, 263, 273,
  284, 308, 311) and b with 16 (2, 22, 47, 52, 62, 65, 78, 92, 96, 121, 135,
  149, 181, 241, 251, 280); check weight 32. Five of the 44 stage-3
  survivors share this divisor.

The GB code has the automorphism (left, i) -> (right, -i mod 337),
(right, j) -> (left, -j mod 337), which maps H_X onto H_Z, so d_X = d_Z and
the other side's witness is the image of the one found. The committed Z
witness is the image of the weight-81 X witness; the GPU's own Z reading was
82. Final claim: d <= 81 on both sides, a witnessed upper bound at
300,000,000 GPU trials per side, the depth the cell's leaders were attacked
at, with two seeds (the second read 82 on X and 83 on Z). The reading fell from 86 at 10,000,000 trials to 81 at
300,000,000, and the board's sibling at this lift fell from 87 to 80 only at
1,000,000,000 trials, so a 1,000,000,000-trial attack is the next thing this
entry should face; every GPU-proposed operator was re-verified on the CPU
before it counted.

## Dead ends

- Z_327: three survivors at k = 150 read 82 at 200,000 trials and
  kd^2/n = 1542, above the headline; at 10,000,000 trials they read 28, 32
  and 40. Those divisors are mixtures across the residue classes mod 3 of
  Z_327 rather than direct sums (checked), so the collapse is a genuine
  light logical, not a decomposition.
- m = 305, 331 and 329 took three quarters of the draws and produced
  nothing that survived 200,000 trials beyond what PR #2810 already filed.
- Of the three Z_337 survivors, [[674,126,84]] (weight 31) and
  [[674,126,86]] (weight 30) passed the gate against the board at their
  10,000,000-trial readings but are dominated in the cell by this entry at
  86; their 300,000,000-trial passes are queued behind this one on the same
  GPU job, and they are not filed.

## Tools

Claude Fable 5.1 (Claude Code) driving the repo kit: research/kit/gf2poly.py
and research/kit/search.py for the construction, research/kit/surrogate.py
(fast backend) for the ladder, research/kit/annihilator.py, and
verify/validate_candidate.py for the gate. The sampler and ladder ran on a
laptop (about 10 hours, 6 threads); the six 10,000,000-trial passes ran in
parallel on one Perlmutter CPU node in 11 minutes (36 threads each, NERSC
project gen0030); the second 10,000,000-trial seed ran on the laptop; the
300,000,000-trial passes ran on one Perlmutter A100 (verify/ris_gpu.py,
about 61 minutes per seed).

## Reproduction

m = 337, H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)], H_Z = [circ(b)^T | circ(a)^T] with the
exponent sets above (circ(a)_{i,j} = 1 iff j - i mod 337 is an exponent of a).
k = 128 by construction, recomputed by the verifier. The JSON carries both
check matrices and both witnesses, so the entry verifies without the search.
